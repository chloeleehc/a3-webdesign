ABOUT ME

Hi, I am Chloe Lee, a Global Business and Digital Art (GBDA) student at the University of Waterloo. A marketing and social media coordinator, photographer, and Graphic Designer.

I am currently the Social Media Coordinator of Haebeat Dance Crew, and Marketing Executive in University of Waterloo Hong Kong Student Association.

Moreover, I own a small (super small) craft business! In my free time, or whenever I feel stressed, I do beadings. It’s a relaxation tool for me to stay away from stressful works. I find myself really into beading months ago, so I decided to start selling my products.

Fun fact: I love everything about apples, and I always sneak apple elements in my graphic design projects!
